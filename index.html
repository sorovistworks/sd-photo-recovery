Get your photos back from an SD card

For photos that were deleted, or lost when the card was formatted. Works on Windows. The card is only read, never changed.

Download Recover Photos Windows 10 or 11 · small file, nothing to install
  1. Put the SD card in the computer.Don't take new photos on it or copy anything onto it first.
  2. Open the file you downloaded, then click Yes.Windows asks for permission because the program has to read the whole card.
  3. Wait. Your photos open in a folder on the Desktop.A large card can take 10 to 30 minutes.
If you see a warning while downloading or opening the file Chrome or Edge: click Keep. A blue “Windows protected your PC” box: click More info, then Run anyway. The warning appears because the program is new, not because anything is wrong with it.
Advanced: scan a card image file instead

If you already copied the card into an image file (for example on a Mac or Linux), open it here. The scan runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

# macOS (find the card with: diskutil list)
sudo dd if=/dev/rdiskN of=~/sdcard.img bs=4m
# Linux (find the card with: lsblk)
sudo dd if=/dev/sdX of=~/sdcard.img bs=4M status=progress
